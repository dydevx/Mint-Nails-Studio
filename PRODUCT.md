# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local customers in and around Bad Homburg who want professional nail care, transparent pricing, visual inspiration, and a fast way to call or navigate to the studio, primarily on mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present Mint Nails Studio as a real, distinctive local beauty atelier. Success means visitors can understand the offer, compare prices, see the studio's work, confirm opening hours, and book by phone or open Google Maps within seconds.

## Brand Personality

Fresh, precise, expressive. The experience should feel feminine and premium without becoming ornate, distant, or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid conventional salon templates, symmetrical split heroes, repeated icon cards, generic botanical decoration, beige luxury styling, glassmorphism, and the predictable editorial formula of serif italics plus tiny labels and ruled columns.

## Design Principles

- Make the mint identity unmistakable, not merely an accent.
- Let real nail imagery and bold composition carry the brand.
- Keep appointment, prices, hours, and directions continuously easy to reach.
- Use asymmetry and pacing to create distinction without sacrificing clarity.
- Preserve factual accuracy and a credible German local-business voice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and gallery controls, descriptive image alternatives, large mobile tap targets, semantic landmarks, and reduced-motion behavior.
